D1 · As-is dataflow (doorbell inside flywheel)

Where does a wake travel today, and what dies with the swarm?

Read it as: the red dashed boundary is the whole problem. Wake, dispatch, and journal share one process and one lifecycle, the only sink is herdr agent.prompt, and anything flywheel didn't spawn (humans, cron, services) has no address.

D2 · After dataflow (plane D)

What changes when the reducer stays and everything around it moves?

Read it as: D is the hub, not another spoke. Flywheel becomes subscriber one with a proxy CLI. Toron, herdr, and br drop below the line as consumed infrastructure. New sinks are effect-layer adapters; the reducer never changes.

D3 · Arrival handshake (discovery without introduction)

How do two harnesses learn each other when nobody mentions anyone?

Read it as: arrival is the handshake. No bind means no arrival (unbound panes are invisible by construction). The operator transcript contains zero pin mentions in E2.

D4 · Wake delivery ladder (one wake, every outcome)

What happens to a single addressed wake from ring to silence?

Read it as: five gates, each with a number. The ladder is identical as-is and after; what changes is who can stand at each end of it.
